# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha73

Alpha73 applies the fixes from three independent reviews of alpha71 (backend, UI, tests and package) together with the owner's reports from the live page.

**Indication rule.** While Auto runs, the light is green. It turns yellow only for a real switch, start or stop, and for a short pulse around each scheduled check (1 s before, 3 s after). It turns red only for a proven failure: a failed channel check, a controller alarm, or no router answer or fresh data for 90 s. The light is dark when the owner turned Auto off. The Auto button and the sidebar Auto lamp always show the same colour.

**Backend:**
- The first Auto Start after an upgrade retries VENDOR-WORKER-BUSY, VENDOR-SETTLING and OBSERVATION-STALE for 90 s with the full preflight, instead of failing on the first try.
- A boost episode is capped at two holds and ends once it has confirmed a failure or switched. After that it rests for one hold, and it never reopens for a channel that has not passed a check since.
- Recovery waits honour Stop. The native supervisor's procd term_timeout is now 300 s.
- The rtp-dispatch stage is recorded only when rtp2 is actually dispatched.
- Each ping sampler keeps its own target, and its cache is cleared when the target changes.
- The shared inventory window is 3 s.
- Removing a stale vendor lock tolerates a race with rtp2's own cleanup.

**UI:**
- Channel lamps keep a route that was confirmed within the last 2 minutes while its proof is only briefly missing. Manual-control decisions never use this memory.
- Late data and transient blockers are time-bounded before they turn red. A disconnected reserve is not treated as transient.
- The sidebar shows a dark lamp and an "off" hint when Auto is off.
- The aria-label follows the signal.
- The countdown is shown only while Auto runs.
- The decision log refreshes at most every 8 s.
- The light block no longer pushes the Step 1 card aside.

**Docs:** CURRENT-STATE describes the ping default, the probe level, the boost and the three-signal light.
