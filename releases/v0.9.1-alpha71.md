# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha71

Alpha71 contains alpha70's stable slider, level ticks, yellow check pulse and wide legend, plus two fixes found on the router right after the alpha70 update.

- **Boost only while Auto watches.** During the Stop/install/Start the WireGuard tunnel was down for a moment, and that opened a 10-minute boost episode. The slider then said level 1 while checks ran every 10 s. A boost episode now starts only while Auto watches the pair (OBSERVING, COMMITTED, DEGRADED-ON-RESERVE). Failures during Start, a switch or a stopped Auto open no episode.
- **The countdown names the boost.** While the boost drives the cadence it reads "Check in 7 s · boost", and an applied level does not override an active boost.

CI source `fa01d084f917199cd1dbc1a6c23ea57fa1e65ad7`: pinned Python 3.11 and 3.9, all suites PASS. The isolated Python 3.9 build is identical.
