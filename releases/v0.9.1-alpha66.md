# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha66

Alpha66 contains the alpha65 probe level, automatic boost and decision log, and all earlier switching fixes. It also fixes a failure observed on the dedicated MT3000.

**What happened on the router.** After an automatic return to the primary (WireGuard), the GL firmware cycled the tunnel down and up and reloaded the firewall three times within eight seconds. The post-switch check failed during that window, so rolling back was the right decision. But both rollback attempts compared kernel fingerprints while the vendor was still rewriting the rules. They refused and used up their bounded budget within seconds, and the pair stayed held. A minute later the kernel was stable and attested.

**The fix.** Before its first kernel check, a pair rollback on the MT3000 now waits, bounded to 60 s, until two conditions hold:
- the GL workers are quiet;
- two kernel fingerprints taken 3 s apart are equal.

Nothing is written while it waits, and the rollback still decides on its own checks. Other boards do not wait. The new package gets one fresh recovery budget, so a router held by this failure recovers by itself after the update.
