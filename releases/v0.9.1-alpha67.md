# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha67

Alpha67 gives the Auto traffic light three signals and stops the page from flashing red while Auto works normally. It contains all alpha60–alpha66 fixes.

Before this release, the overview regularly turned red ("traffic channel not verified · KERNEL-POLICY-UNVERIFIED") and then back to green, while the controller was OBSERVING and both channels passed their checks. The router's kernel route proof is valid for 15 s. One controller cycle, a 4–6 s status call and the 10 s page poll often take longer than that, so the proof expired between updates and the page showed it as a failure.

- **Three lamps.**
  - Green: Auto works on a confirmed route.
  - Yellow: the router is re-checking the route or a channel, or is switching, starting or stopping. Auto keeps running.
  - Red: a check of the selected channel completed with an error, the controller reported an alarm, the router program does not answer, or Auto is off.
- **Held confirmation.** A route proof that has only expired is held for up to 60 s after the last confirmation. A route the router explicitly reports as unverified is never held.
- **Button and sidebar lamp.** The Auto button and the sidebar lamp follow the same signal. They stay "working" while the light is yellow and turn red only on a real failure.
- **Faster test suite.** A shared MT3000 test fixture no longer runs the real bounded settle wait in every rollback; that wait has its own tests.

Alpha67: у светофора Auto три сигнала.
- Зелёный: Auto работает, канал подтверждён.
- Жёлтый: идёт проверка или переключение, Auto при этом работает.
- Красный: настоящий сбой или Auto выключен.

Обычная фоновая перепроверка больше не окрашивает страницу в красный: подтверждённый маршрут удерживается до 60 секунд.
