# HomenS.AI Auto VPN 1.0.0-beta3

1.0.0-beta3 adds a fast channel switch.

- **Fast switch.** Instead of the GL rebuild (/usr/bin/rtp2.sh), the committed route_policy order is written to the kernel in one atomic step. The step replaces the tunnel order in mangle ROUTE_POLICY and the router's own VPN-process rule, then drops the flows of the channel being left.
- **Measured on the MT3000, 30 Oct 2026:**
  - the rule change takes 8–33 ms;
  - a LAN test client was on the new channel within 0.5 s;
  - no request failed and DNS kept working;
  - there is no firewall reload and no dnsmasq restart.
- **For comparison, the GL rebuild:** the rebuild alone takes 20–33 s, and the whole switch 53–60 s.
- **Fallback.** If the firewall chains are not exactly as expected, the switch uses the GL rebuild as before. The owner can turn the fast switch off with `option fast_switch '0'`.
- **Unchanged.** Total time from a fault is still dominated by failure detection (about 105 s) until faster detection lands in the next version.
- **Monitoring page.** A new Monitoring page opens from the sidebar lamp. It shows a small lamp, the probe level and per-channel checks for the last hour.
- **Help.** A new topic, "How Auto works", explains switching and return with a diagram.
- **Charts.** Points on the delay charts are joined across measurement periods.
