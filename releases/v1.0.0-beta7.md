# HomenS.AI Auto VPN 1.0.0-beta7

1.0.0-beta7 is the fast-switching release. Betas 3 to 6 were internal test builds and were not published.

- **Fast channel switch.** The committed route_policy order is written to the kernel in one atomic step: the tunnel order in mangle ROUTE_POLICY and the router's own VPN-process rule. Flows of the channel being left are then dropped. There is no GL rebuild (rtp2), no firewall reload and no dnsmasq restart.
  - Measured on the MT3000 on 30 Oct 2026: the rule change takes 8–33 ms. A LAN client was on the new channel within 0.5 s, with no failed request.
  - In real Auto switches the whole transaction, including safety checks, took about 30 s to the reserve and about 25 s back, against 53–60 s and about 45 s with the GL rebuild.
  - Any unexpected chain falls back to the GL rebuild. The fast switch can be turned off in Settings.
- **Faster failure detection.** Every ~2 s the router watches the kernel traffic counters of both Auto channels and the WireGuard handshake. If a channel sends but receives nothing for 6 s, dense full checks start every 4 s, on both channels. Auto still switches only after the usual number of failed full checks.
- **In-memory status.** The monitor answers the page's status requests from memory over a root-only local socket, and the rpcd plugin uses a tiny client for them. If anything goes wrong it falls back to the old path.
- **Recovery fixes found by the device tests:**
  - The flush of old flows no longer goes through the fenced kernel writer.
  - Recovery accepts a target that our own kernel writer has already applied.
  - Post-switch verification waits up to 20 s for a busy GL worker instead of rolling back a correct switch.
- **New in the interface:**
  - a Monitoring page;
  - a "How Auto works" diagram in Help;
  - joined chart lines;
  - a fast-switch checkbox in Settings.

1.0.0-beta7 — быстрое переключение.
- **Быстрое переключение.** Порядок каналов записывается в ядро одним атомарным изменением (8–33 мс), без перестройки GL, перезагрузки firewall и DNS. Полное переключение со всеми проверками в Auto: около 30 с на резерв и около 25 с обратно (раньше 53–60 с и около 45 с).
- **Быстрое обнаружение сбоя.** Роутер следит за счётчиками трафика и handshake WireGuard и сразу запускает плотные проверки.
- **Статус в памяти.** Страница получает статус почти без нагрузки на роутер.
- **Исправления восстановления**, найденные на роутере.
- **В интерфейсе:**
  - страница «Мониторинг»;
  - схема «Логика работы Auto» в помощи;
  - соединённые точки на графиках;
  - переключатель «Быстрое переключение» в настройках.
