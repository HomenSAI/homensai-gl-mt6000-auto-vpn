# HomenS.AI Auto VPN 2.0.0-alpha13 (Lua line, test build)

**Runtime:** the separate Lua line (branch `feat/lua-core-20261030`). The table of what runs on Lua 5.1 and what on Python 3.9 is the same as in 2.0.0-alpha12, plus: history and accounting are written by Lua with `lua_monitor=1` (reliability model, 24 h route, delay history, the statistics reset); the site measurement and the comparison of response methods are not ported and are not written with the Lua monitor.

- **License transition (31.10.2026), as in 1.0.0-beta14:** HomenSAI Personal & Commercial Source License v1.0 (`LicenseRef-HomenSAI-Personal-Commercial-1.0`) in `LICENSE`, the package metadata and the installed license copies; MIT history kept in `LICENSE-MIT-LEGACY`; own source headers point to `LICENSE`. **Operational terms version 2:** an earlier acceptance no longer counts, Auto stays off and refuses to start (also the automatic resume after a power cut) until the owner accepts the new terms on the page.
- **History and accounting on Lua (plan item 6):** the Lua monitor wrote `checkpoint.json` without the reliability model and dropped it from the backup too, so incident history, monthly probe/failure counters and the 24 h route were lost after two saves (also for the Python line, which restores from the same files). The model is kept again, byte-compatible with Python. The statistics reset queued by the page is applied by the Lua monitor (it waited forever). In diagnostics mode the delay history is written again and switch events carry their latency again.
- **Terms gate fix:** the pair Auto and the MT3000 platform (Python) and the Lua backend/pair compared the accepted terms with a literal `1`; with version 2 accepted the pair Auto would never have started. All gates now use the one terms version (`lua/terms.lua` tested equal to `agent.TERMS_VERSION`).
- **Emergency tool and the Lua supervisor:** `homensai-vpn-emergency close-journal` now also refuses while `lua/auto.lua` runs, and `stop-auto` stops the services again after its configuration commit (the commit's trigger restarted them). Found while recovering the MT3000 from the alpha11 power-cut dead end.
- Measured on the MT3000 with alpha12 after the recovery: Auto started from Start in 15 s and returned to the primary by itself 78 s later (PRIMARY-STABLY-RECOVERED, client confirmed).
