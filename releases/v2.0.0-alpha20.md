# HomenS.AI Auto VPN 2.0.0-alpha20 (Lua line, test build)

**Runtime:** as 2.0.0-alpha19. Default: two Lua processes (monitor and Auto); `hv-core` stays an option.

- **The page no longer waits during switches (MT3000):** the monitor decoded the ~1 MB switch-history file in its event loop after every write of the Auto supervisor (1–4 s each; a switch writes it several times). A child process (`history_public.lua`) now builds the page's history projection and returns it as a Lua table constructor that the monitor loads with the C lexer. Until the child finishes, the previous projection is served (a few seconds old). The first projection, and any projection after a failed child, is built in the monitor as before.

**Measured on the MT3000** (manual switches to the reserve and back, a `status` call every 1 s, no profiler):

| | two processes, alpha19 | two processes, alpha20 | hv-core, alpha20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `status` over 1 s during switches | 6 of 40, 6 of 40 | 0 of 46, 0 of 47 | 1 of 47 |
| Longest `status` during switches | 2.0–2.2 s | 0.42–0.50 s | 1.2 s |
| Traffic moved / switch done | 1.4–1.6 / 2.5–2.9 s | 1.4–1.7 / 2.6–3.4 s | 1.7–2.2 / 3.3–4.4 s |
| CPU, own + children | 34.7–40.6 % | 44.1–51.5 % (the history child: 19–26 %) | 47.6 % |

The page now answers within 0.5 s during switches. The price is the child's CPU while the history changes.
