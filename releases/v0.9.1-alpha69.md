# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha69

Alpha69 fixes the indication on top of alpha68's batch: the overview no longer stays yellow while Auto works normally.

We measured on the MT3000: the monitor publishes about every 10.5 s, and the controller renews the kernel route proof every ~5 s, about 1 s old at publication. The page receives an answer only every 10–20 s, but it kept ageing the content of the last answer by its own clock while it waited. A proof that was fresh when the router served it therefore looked expired most of the time.

- **Freshness is judged at the answer time.** The page checks once whether the router's whole answer is fresh. It judges the route proof and the controller heartbeat inside that answer at the time the router served it.
- **Green between routine checks.** A scheduled routine check is green, not yellow. Yellow means a real delay, a switch or a transient blocker.
- **The countdown keeps running.** "Check in N s" follows the router's schedule and keeps cycling between page updates.
- **The Auto button matches its label.** Its colour now follows the same signal as the label: no red button under "Auto works".
- **Shorter wording:** "Route check", "Updating data", "No answer, retrying".
