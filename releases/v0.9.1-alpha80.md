# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha80

Alpha80 makes every indication follow one rule, keeps Auto running after a manual choice, always shows a channel delay and adds an optional comparison of all response methods. Alpha74 to alpha79 were internal builds and were never published.

**One rule for every lamp.**
- The sidebar, Overview, manual cards, channel cards, the server table, the Tests page and the live journal show the same colours.
- Green means the channel carries your traffic.
- Yellow means the channel is alive and not in use. Only that channel offers a switch to it.
- Red means a proven failure. Grey means the router has not answered yet.
- A generated test of 34,560 status combinations (4,640 rendered through the real page code) checks that the button, the traffic light, the lamps and the journal always agree. It found six inconsistencies, all fixed.

**Manual choice never turns Auto off.**
- The "Resume Auto" button is gone.
- Auto keeps watching the chosen channel. If that channel fails, Auto moves traffic to the other one.
- During a switch the destination card blinks green, and both cards keep the same height.

**Delays are always shown.**
- Without a current measurement, the last known delay is shown with its age. The delay recommendation uses it too.
- While ping is the metric, the chart and statistics use their own ping history.
- The response history could not be saved after a manual switch, because the route kinds "blocked" and "down" failed validation. Fixed.

**Measure all methods (Settings, optional).** Each check also pings the other DNS addresses and opens the three websites. The Tests ranking then shows one column per method. Switching still uses only the chosen method.

**Switch countdown.** The router keeps the average confirmed switch time on flash, with one small write per switch. The countdown shows "about N s (usual M s)" for manual and automatic switches alike.

**Readable codes and journal.**
- 115 owner-readable code descriptions in English and Russian, for example PAIR-MANUAL-HOLD-PRIMARY/RESERVE: "channel chosen by you; Auto keeps watching".
- The journal says what Auto was doing, never "channel switch not recorded".

**Lower load.** The status call shares one kernel attestation every 15 s, so one call takes about 2 s of MT3000 CPU instead of 7 s.

**Layout.**
- A large Monitoring lamp sits beside the Auto button.
- "Step 1" now reads "To check the VPNs by hand, press the button."
- The chart legend shows the server name with its mean on a second line.

**Docs.** The documentation and the built-in help were rewritten for the current behaviour, with English and Russian checked against each other. A new help topic explains the probe level and the automatic boost in detail.
