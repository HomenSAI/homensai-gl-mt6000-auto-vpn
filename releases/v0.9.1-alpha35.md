# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha35

Auto switching rules are configurable in Settings. Default values: checks every 10 s; three primary failures and two successful reserve checks; 60 s cooldown and reserve dwell; at most three attempts per 15 minutes; two successful primary checks and 30 s stability before return. A verified primary can return earlier when the reserve fails. Existing settings and 15-minute attempt history are preserved. Stop Auto before saving changes and restart it afterward.

Immutable CI passed in Python 3.11 and 3.9, including 846 application and 113 QA tests in each, plus 64 UI suites in the first environment. Four native Chromium cases used synthetic RPC. Dedicated GL-MT3000 was upgraded, the previous Auto ON state restored and both channels passed router DNS and HTTPS checks. Custom values were saved and read back in an isolated router configuration; live settings were untouched. There was no injected failover or external LAN test. Pre/post encrypted backups were checked. Stable main/latest remain unchanged.

---

В настройках доступны интервалы, пороги, паузы, лимит попыток и условия возврата. По умолчанию сохранены значения 10 с, 3/2 проверки, 60 с, 3 попытки за 15 минут и 2 проверки / 30 с. Для сохранения сначала остановите Auto, затем снова включите его. Обновление установлено на выделенный MT3000; Auto и оба VPN работают по проверкам роутера.
