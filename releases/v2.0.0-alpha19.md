# HomenS.AI Auto VPN 2.0.0-alpha19 (Lua line, test build)

**Runtime:** as 2.0.0-alpha18. Default: two Lua processes (monitor and Auto); the one-process `hv-core` stays an option.

- **Manual and automatic switches are 2.5–3 times faster (MT3000 profile):** writing the switch history was about 70 % of the Lua Auto supervisor's CPU. Every time old events fell out of the retention window, the whole history was encoded 4–5 times and the in-memory copy dropped, so the next event decoded the file again. Now each write encodes the history once and keeps the copy. The monitor reuses its parsed copy for unchanged files instead of decoding the file on every page read. In hv-core, the monitor and Auto share one copy and no longer evict each other's. File contents are unchanged (byte-identical to the Python writer).

**Measured on the MT3000** (manual switches to the reserve and back, a `status` call every 1 s, no profiler; two rounds of two switches each):

| | two processes, alpha16 | two processes, alpha19 | hv-core, alpha17+fixes | hv-core, alpha19 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Traffic moved | 3.7 s | 1.4–1.6 s | 7.1–8.3 s | 1.5–2.4 s |
| Switch done | 7.2–7.4 s | 2.5–2.9 s | 15.5–16.9 s | 3.1–5.8 s |
| `status` over 1 s during switches | 8 of 49 | 6 of 40 (both rounds) | 24 of 50 | 0 of 47, 1 of 48 |
| CPU (processes + children) | 47.6 % | 34.7–40.6 % | 53.0 % | 29.3–30.5 % |
| RSS | 35.8 MB | 34–37 MB | 25.0 MB | 22–26 MB |

Two processes switch fastest; hv-core keeps the page more responsive and uses less CPU and memory.

2.0.0-alpha19 — отдельная линия Lua, тестовая сборка.

Переключения стали в 2,5–3 раза быстрее: запись истории переключений занимала около 70 % процессора Lua Auto (вся история перекодировалась 4–5 раз на каждое событие). Теперь — один раз, а прочитанная копия хранится в памяти. На MT3000 при двух службах трафик уходит на другой канал за 1,4–1,6 с (было 3,7 с), переключение готово за 2,5–2,9 с (было 7,2–7,4 с). Один процесс ядра (hv-core): 1,5–2,4 с и 3,1–5,8 с, страница при этом отвечает быстрее, процессора и памяти меньше. По умолчанию — две службы.
