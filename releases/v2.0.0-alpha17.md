# HomenS.AI Auto VPN 2.0.0-alpha17 (Lua line, test build)

**Runtime:** as 2.0.0-alpha16 (Python 3.9 base and fallback; Lua 5.1 monitor and Auto as two processes by default). New: the one-process `hv-core` (`lua_core_process='1'`) keeps its event loop responsive while Auto switches.

- **hv-core no longer holds the page during a switch:** on alpha14 the Auto step held the shared loop 6–10 s. Auto's router commands (uci, iptables, nft, ifup, the probe bridge, lock helpers) now run as loop children, and its sleeps under its own locks and its DNS check yield to the loop. In tests the longest Auto step went from 3.0 s to under 0.01 s and a `status` call waited at most 0.01 s. New counters in `hv-core.json`: `slow_auto_steps`, `max_loop_lag_s`, `max_rpc_s`, `async_commands` and others; a step of 0.5 s or more is logged as `HV-CORE-AUTO-STEP`. The two-process mode is unchanged.
- **Power cut during a switch (24 h soak finding, alpha16 cycle 12):** right after boot the vendor still reloads the firewall, so the recovery rollback failed twice with `KERNEL-POLICY-UNVERIFIED`. The result was `ROLLBACK-GIVEN-UP`, a `RESTORING` journal, and Start refused. In the first 600 s of uptime these failures (`KERNEL-POLICY-UNVERIFIED`, `ROLLBACK-UNVERIFIED`) are now retried every 20 s and not counted (`ROLLBACK-BOOT-RETRY` in the log). Later failures, other codes and failures after Stop count as before. The rollback checks themselves are unchanged. Python and Lua behave the same.
- **Service stop reaches the Lua Auto supervisor:** while Lua Auto waited inside a pass (for example in the `ROLLBACK-GIVEN-UP` alarm), `/etc/init.d/homensai-vpn stop` did not end it until procd killed it 300 s later. `stop_service` now leaves `/var/run/homensai-vpn/service-stop.flag`, which Lua Auto reads.

2.0.0-alpha17 — отдельная линия Lua, тестовая сборка.

Один процесс ядра (hv-core) больше не задерживает страницу во время переключения: команды Auto выполняются, не останавливая общий цикл. Отключение питания посреди переключения больше не заводит в тупик: в первые 10 минут после загрузки откат повторяется, пока производитель перестраивает firewall. Остановка службы теперь сразу останавливает Lua Auto.

**Ядро:** как в alpha16; hv-core доработан, но по умолчанию по-прежнему две службы.
