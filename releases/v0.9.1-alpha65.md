# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha65

Alpha65 adds an owner-selected probe level and an automatic boost. Router load can now be traded against failure-detection speed instead of probing at one fixed rate. It contains all alpha60–alpha64 fixes.

On Overview, under "Automatic VPN failover":

- **Probe level slider, 1 to 5.** One end means fewer checks and lower load; the other means more checks and faster switching. Under the slider the page shows the cadence and the expected time to confirm a failure for the selected level.

  | Level | Channel check | Ping and websites |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | every 60 s | every 5 min |
  | 2 | every 30 s | every 2 min |
  | 3 | every 20 s | every 60 s |
  | 4 | every 15 s | every 45 s |
  | 5 | every 10 s | every 30 s |

  Installations that never chose a level keep their previous cadence (10 s = level 5). The level can be changed while Auto runs, except during a switch, a recovery or a speed test.

- **Automatic boost checkbox (on by default), with its description.** When the primary or reserve channel fails a check, the router checks every 10 s and measures every 30 s for 10 minutes. Every further failure extends that window. After 10 minutes without failures the router returns to the chosen level. A reliable pair is therefore checked rarely, while a real failure is still confirmed quickly. An active boost is shown on the page with the channel and the code.

- **Decision log ("levels and boost").** Each row shows the time, the level, the decision, a short reason and the code. Rows are written for every level choice and for the start and end of every boost episode. An episode ends with one of four results:
  - the channel was switched;
  - the failure was confirmed;
  - false alarm;
  - the owner turned the boost off.

  The log keeps at most 500 rows and writes to flash only when a decision happens.

Ping and website samplers take their cadence from the level. Each result stays current for 1.5 cadences plus 15 s.

Alpha65 добавляет на «Обзоре» под автоматическим переключением VPN ползунок «Уровень опроса» от 1 до 5, то есть выбор между нагрузкой на роутер и скоростью обнаружения сбоя. Под ползунком — чекбокс «Автоматическое ускорение» с описанием. При неудачной проверке канала роутер на 10 минут переходит на проверку раз в 10 секунд, затем возвращается к выбранному уровню. Каждое решение (уровень, начало и итог ускорения, код ошибки) записывается в «Журнал решений». Пакет 986153 байт, SHA256 `9581a4105469d23a414704857264b959b133d22c0d0321eb853479e9cd7c0282`.
