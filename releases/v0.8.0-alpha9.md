Package 0.8.0-alpha9-1, dashboard50/ui28. Not accepted on the physical router.

- Training page: eight lesson cards with examples, an eight-question quiz with right/wrong highlighting, a score out of 100 and "take again".
- Overview: one summary card (colour + one sentence + working VPNs) and a "Getting started" checklist; "Step 1 check the VPNs / Step 2 turn on Auto" wording.
- Settings: simple settings (System, Telegram, monitor Start/Stop with its own traffic light) and a collapsed "Advanced settings" for administrators; per-step guides with examples; Tests moved under Advanced (with the speed-test settings).
- Servers are shown as country + large protocol (OpenVPN / WireGuard); Channels page explains where the list comes from; page scrolls to the top when you open another page.
- Help: step-by-step Telegram bot setup (BotFather, token, chat ID, error codes). New docs/HOW-IT-WORKS.en.md / .ru.md.
- One design layer (cards, headings, buttons) and a colour-theme switch (system / light / dark).

Limits: virtual and model checks cannot certify GL.iNet workers, LAN DNS/Kill Switch/IPv6 or power-loss behaviour; unattended operation is not supported.
