# HomenS.AI Auto VPN 1.0.0-beta1

1.0.0-beta1 is the first stable beta. Two VPN channels (primary and reserve) are watched by the router itself. When the primary fails, traffic is switched to the reserve, and it goes back to the primary when the primary has recovered. In the controlled-fault tests on 30 Oct 2026, every switch that Auto started completed and returned to the primary: 20 faults, 19 switches. The one fault without a switch was refused on purpose by the switch budget.

## How long a switch takes (measured)

Each test fault blocked the traffic of the primary WireGuard on a GL.iNet GL-MT3000; the reserve was OpenVPN over TCP. All times run from the start of the failure.

| Probe level | Faults | Failure noticed | Traffic on the reserve: fastest / mean / slowest |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (60 s checks, boost always on) | 8 | 47–73 s | **160 / 171 / 183 s** |
| 3 (20 s checks + boost) | 4 | 27–34 s | **150 / 153 / 163 s** |
| 4 (15 s checks + boost) | 4 | 26–38 s | **140 / 154 / 159 s** |
| 5 (10 s checks + boost) | 3 of 4 | 27–33 s | **152 / 157 / 162 s** |

**Expected range from a failure to working traffic on the reserve: 140–185 s (about 2.5–3 minutes).**

- The switch itself took 46–76 s, 63 s on average, at every level. During that time the GL firmware rebuilds its VPN routing.
- After the primary recovers, Auto waits about 45 s for it to stay stable. It then returns to the primary in 38–57 s (48 s on average).
- When failures repeat more often than the switch budget allows (several per hour), Auto pauses switching on purpose. In the test with a fault every 5 minutes, one switch was refused this way. This protects the network from endless flapping.
- Higher probe levels notice a failure about twice as fast (about 30 s instead of about 60 s). The total stays about 2.5 minutes, because confirming the failure and proving the reserve takes about 55 s, and the switch itself takes about 60 s.

A new switching core is being designed to cut the total time to about 15–30 s. See `docs/architecture/NEW-CORE-2026-10-30.ru.md`. All figures in it are estimates until the device experiments are done.

## What is new since 0.9.1-alpha73

**Indication**
- One rule for every lamp on every page, checked by a generated matrix of status combinations.
- Green means the channel carries your traffic. Blinking yellow means a switch is running or the router is confirming the route. Red means a proven failure. Grey means no router data.

**Switching**
- A manual choice never turns Auto off. The "Resume Auto" button is gone. If the chosen channel fails, Auto moves traffic to the other one.

**Probe levels and countdown**
- Probe level 1–5. Levels 1 and 2 always use the automatic boost; at levels 3–5 you choose.
- The switch countdown uses the measured average switch time plus 25%.

**Delay and response measurement**
- A channel always shows its delay: the last measured value and its age.
- The ping history is saved, and the delay chart follows the response metric you chose.
- Optional "measure all methods" comparison with one column per method in the ranking.

**Lower load**
- One status call now takes about 2 s of router CPU instead of 7 s.
- The long event lists come from a separate history call instead of every 10 s poll.

**Fixes found by the fault tests**
- The controlled-fault test on the Tests page never worked in two-channel mode: it deadlocked on the control lock and also miscompared the kernel proof time. Both are fixed.

**Help and documentation**
- New help topic "Measured results of Auto mode", with phase charts and tables.
- Owner-readable descriptions for 115 more codes.
- The English interface was checked on every page.
- Documentation and help were rewritten for the current behaviour.

**Layout**
- The sidebar lamps are links: Auto opens Overview, Monitoring opens its statistics, Primary and Reserve open the channel cards.
- The monitoring lamp was removed from the Auto card.

## HomenS.AI Auto VPN 1.0.0-beta1 (RU)

Первая стабильная бета. Роутер сам следит за двумя VPN-каналами, основным и резервным. При отказе основного трафик переключается на резерв, а после восстановления основного возвращается на него. В тестах 30.10.2026 было 20 контролируемых сбоев и 19 переключений: каждое начатое переключение завершилось, и трафик вернулся на основной канал. Один сбой прошёл без переключения: его намеренно отклонил лимит переключений.

**Время от поломки до работы через резерв: 140–185 с (около 2,5–3 минут).**

| Уровень опроса | Быстрее всего | Среднее | Дольше всего |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (ускорение всегда) | 160 с | 171 с | 183 с |
| 3 | 150 с | 153 с | 163 с |
| 4 | 140 с | 154 с | 159 с |
| 5 | 152 с | 157 с | 162 с |

- Само переключение занимает 46–76 с, в среднем 63 с: прошивка GL перестраивает маршрутизацию VPN.
- Возврат на основной канал занимает 38–57 с. Он начинается примерно через 45 с после того, как основной канал восстановился и работает стабильно.
- На уровнях 3–5 сбой замечается примерно за 30 с, на уровне 1 — примерно за 60 с.
- Новое ядро должно сократить полное время примерно до 15–30 с. Это пока оценка, она будет проверяться опытами на роутере.
