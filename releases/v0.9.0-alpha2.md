# v0.9.0-alpha2 - failure analysis, controller and Stop hardening

> **Experimental alpha software: use at your own risk.** It can interrupt your internet connection and changes router VPN settings. Provided "AS IS" without warranty (MIT License). You install and use it entirely at your own risk and responsibility. Back up first and keep another way to reach the internet and the router. Read DISCLAIMER.md. The program shows the same terms on first start and does not allow Auto until you accept them.

Package 0.9.0-alpha2-1, dashboard65/ui43. Not accepted on the physical router.

- Failure matrix (offline, simulated router): hung vendor apply, missing or wrong client confirmation, Stop and service stop during a switch, exit change, late blockers, failing and unverifiable rollback, supervisor restart in every phase, foreign policy change, corrupt journal, malformed exit replies, Stop with unreadable settings, settings writer hang or refusal. 26 tests; the full report is docs/FAILURE-ANALYSIS-0.9.0-alpha2.md.
- Fixed: a cut, garbled or non-IPv4 exit reply is now a coded refusal (EXIT-TRACE-FAILED) instead of ending the switching service.
- Fixed: Stop always writes its latch, even if the router settings cannot be read, and always cleans up a running failure test (STOP-UNCONFIRMED when the latch could not be saved).
- Fixed: a hung or missing settings writer answers SETUP-SAVE-UNCONFIRMED instead of an error without a code.
- Page: the chosen design is kept for the session when the browser blocks storage; check marks, step chips and profile details are readable in dark themes and in design C. New offline page probe in qa/ui-probe/ (every section in every design, themes, languages, widths, clicks, picker persistence).
- Remaining risks that need an owner decision are listed in the report (corrupt journal and repeated failed rollback both block the LAN for up to 90 s per supervisor start).
- Includes 0.9.0-alpha1 and earlier.
