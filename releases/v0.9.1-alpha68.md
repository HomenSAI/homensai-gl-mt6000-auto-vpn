# Auto VPN 0.9.1-alpha68

Alpha68 delivers the owner's batch of 29 Oct 2026 in one package (see `docs/handoff/BATCH-TASKS-2026-10-29.ru.md`). It contains all alpha60–alpha67 fixes.

## One Auto signal everywhere
The Auto button, the traffic light and the sidebar group (Auto, Monitoring, Primary, Reserve) use the same rule:

- **Green:** Auto works on a freshly confirmed route.
- **Yellow, button stays "Auto works":** something is in progress or late, but nothing has failed:
  - the router is re-confirming the route;
  - a scheduled check is due or the channels are switching;
  - a transient controller condition (such as stale observations or a settling tunnel);
  - late data, or no answer from the router for up to 90 s.
- **Red:** only a real failure:
  - a critical controller alarm;
  - a channel check that completed with an error;
  - no answer from the router for more than 90 s.
- **Dark light and grey lamp:** you turned Auto off.

Also:
- A countdown under the light shows the time to the next scheduled check, which follows the probe level (and the boost).
- The technical state no longer changes font on refresh.

## Probe level
- The slider shows the level saved on the router, so it no longer jumps between levels.
- After a level change the monitor checks at once. A faster level therefore no longer causes a brief "observation stale" block.
- The decision log keeps its rows instead of reloading, stays open across reloads and shows error codes on one line.

## Response measurement (Settings)
- **Default: ping to 1.1.1.1.** It barely loads the router and is the best way to compare the two channels. 8.8.8.8 and 9.9.9.9 are also available.
- **Three websites over HTTPS:** closer to opening real pages, but it loads the router more.
- When ping is selected, the website check does not run at all.
- One response model feeds every place on the page.
- Help has a new topic, "How the response is measured".

## Lighter monitor
- While Telegram is off, the monitor starts no Telegram thread, does no Lua read and makes no status copy.
- The samplers share one settings read every 10 s.
- The page's status answer leaves out per-sample tunnel identity fields the page never reads.
- The simple home page shows the two responses side by side.
