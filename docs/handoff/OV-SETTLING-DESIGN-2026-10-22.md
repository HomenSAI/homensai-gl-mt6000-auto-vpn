# Offline tunnel-readiness model — 22 October 2026

This is an isolated evidence reducer in `private/ov-settling-model-20261022`, not installed code, a new router guard or a replacement for the existing observer. No physical calls or threshold changes.

A connected flag and empty queue are insufficient. A potential readiness witness must bind boot ID, profile identity, daemon PID/start and kernel ifindex to one generation. It must verify actual selected route state, absence of in-flight workers/queues, and genuine completion of the required service work. Queue disappearance cannot supply that last proof: stock consumers remove entries even after failed reload.

The model requires at least four continuously sampled observations over three seconds, gaps no greater than1.5seconds, unchanged route, and a fresh same-generation/same-route DNS+HTTPS result taken during that window and no more than two seconds old. READY_ROUTER_LOCAL is a point-in-time result; a later busy sample revokes it. Identity drift, hard fault, stale/reordered timestamp or deadline produces sticky HOLD. Route changes and observation gaps reset the window. Original recursion/retry/memory/lock/deadline and independent cleanup safeguards are not altered.

Seven synthetic scenarios pass: nominal current evidence, connected/queue insufficiency, fault/deadline/identity drift, route change, observation gap, old/late/foreign probe and busy-window reset. They prove only reducer behavior. The three-second window is an offline design parameter, not calibrated hardware acceptance.

Blocking collector work: `reloads_verified` is an explicit missing proof, not a value to infer from an empty queue or substitute true. No existing collector currently supplies the whole evidence contract. Require strict schema/finite typed values, monotonic device timestamps, bounded snapshot/probe timing and stable identity before/after probes. Capture only coded metadata, never credentials, raw environment or profile contents. Define whether completion is measured directly or through independently verified final runtime effect; make that distinction explicit. Native/app Auto and LAN acceptance remain open.
