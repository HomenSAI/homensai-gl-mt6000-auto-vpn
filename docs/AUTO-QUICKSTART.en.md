# Quick start 0.9.0

1. [Install the verified package](INSTALL.en.md), open Services → HomenS.AI Auto VPN and refresh Ctrl+F5.
2. If terms are not accepted, read and tick the confirmations. Read the three operating diagrams next and press OK.
3. Review the [eight steps](FIRST-RUN-SETUP.md), then save. The bottom green escape preserves existing settings without saving the draft; resume later through Settings → All application settings.
4. Check monitoring and both selected VPNs. Press the round Auto button once and wait for readiness; it locks during Start/Stop.
5. While Auto is on, an eligible red card lets you select the other channel. A healthy green selected card animates. A queued acknowledgement is not completion.
6. To stop, use the round button and wait for confirmation. Restart requires explicit Auto Start again.

Live and stored1–24hour charts show route selection, not speed. Confirmed1→2/2→1 events, detailed week, monthly aggregates and lifetime counters are available. Two Connected tunnels do not mean two selected common-traffic routes. [Evidence scope and limits](CURRENT-STATUS.md).
