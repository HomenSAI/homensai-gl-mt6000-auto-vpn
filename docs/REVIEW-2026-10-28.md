## Strengths

Strict RPC decoding, no shell injection; checksummed storage with backups and O_NOFOLLOW; kernel-timeout safety nets and a journal-first transaction; reproducible package builds checked on two runtimes; isolated CI; guarded publication; a large error catalogue linked from every code.
